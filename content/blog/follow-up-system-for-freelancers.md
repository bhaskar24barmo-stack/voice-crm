title: A Simple Follow-Up System for Freelancers and Consultants
description: A lightweight routine to make sure no lead or client goes quiet, without buying complicated software.
date: 2026-10-21
---
Freelancers rarely lose work to competitors. They lose it to silence: a proposal sent, then forgotten. A simple system fixes that.

## Decide your follow-up rhythm
Pick default gaps and stick to them, for example a check-in two days after a proposal, then a week later, then a final polite close-out.

## Always leave a conversation with a next step
End every call by naming who does what by when. Write it down straight away.

## Keep one list
Whether it is a spreadsheet or a CRM, keep every lead and client in one place with three fields: status, last contact, next action.

## Make follow-ups useful
Do not just ask “any update?” Add something small: a relevant idea, a question, or a clearer next option.

## Do a weekly sweep
Once a week, sort by last contact and message anyone you have not touched in the agreed gap.
