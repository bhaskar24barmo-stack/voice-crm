title: Voice Notes vs Typed Notes for Customer Conversations
description: When speaking your notes works better than typing them, and how to keep voice notes organized and useful.
date: 2026-10-21
---
Both methods work. The right choice depends on where you are and what you need afterwards.

## When voice notes win
- You are on the move, driving between visits, or only have a phone
- You want to capture detail quickly before it fades
- Typing on a small screen slows you down

## When typed notes win
- You need to scan or search quickly later
- You are in a quiet shared space
- The content is sensitive and should not be spoken aloud

## The weakness of raw voice notes
A recording alone is hard to search and easy to forget. The value comes from turning speech into structured records: a contact, a summary, and a dated next step.

## A good habit
Speak a short summary right after each conversation, then review it before it is saved. A quick check catches mishearings and keeps your data trustworthy.
