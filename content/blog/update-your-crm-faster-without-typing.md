Most CRMs fall out of date for one reason: updating them is a chore that happens after the real work. Here is how to make it smaller.

## 1. Capture while the conversation is fresh
Details fade fast. Record the key points within minutes of a call or meeting, not at the end of the day.

## 2. Use a fixed note shape
Write every note with the same four parts:
- Who you spoke with
- What they need
- What was agreed
- The next step and its date

## 3. Speak instead of type
Most people talk much faster than they type, and speaking works well on a phone between meetings. Dictating a short summary is often the quickest way to get a note down.

## 4. Turn every next step into a task
A note without a follow-up date is just a memory. Add the task at the same moment you write the note.

## 5. Review once a day
Spend five minutes scanning today’s notes and tomorrow’s tasks. Small, regular cleanups beat a painful monthly catch-up.
