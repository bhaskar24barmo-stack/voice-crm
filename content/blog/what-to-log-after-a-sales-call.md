Good call notes let anyone continue the conversation. Bad ones are either empty or a wall of text. Aim for the middle.

## The essentials
- **Contact and company:** the full name and role of everyone involved
- **Reason for the call:** what prompted it and what they hoped to get
- **Needs and pain points:** in their words where possible
- **Budget, timing, and decision makers:** whatever you learned, and what you still do not know
- **Objections:** what held them back
- **Next step:** the action, the owner, and the date

## What to leave out
Skip small talk and anything you would not want the customer to read. Notes should be factual and respectful.

## Keep it consistent
A repeatable template makes notes searchable and comparable. Over time it also shows which questions you forget to ask.

## Log it quickly
The longer you wait, the less you remember. Capture the main points straight after the call, even if it is a rough summary.
